How it is calculated

  • Gross pension = average emoluments × qualifying years × 7/300 (30 years gives 70%).
  • Retirement on/after 1 July 2024: average of the last 24 months. Earlier: last 12 months.
  • Minimum 10 years of qualifying service for a pension; below that a gratuity is paid. Service above 30 years is not counted.
  • Up to 35% of gross pension may be commuted; the rest is the net monthly pension.

This tool gives an estimate for guidance only. Pension rules change; always confirm with the Finance Division notifications and your Accounts Office / AGPR.
